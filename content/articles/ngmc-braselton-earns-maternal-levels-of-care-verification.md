Northeast Georgia Medical Center (NGMC) Braselton recently earned The Joint Commission's Maternal Levels of Care Verification designation as a Level II Maternity Center, Northeast Georgia Health System announced on October 29.

## What the Designation Means

The Joint Commission's program evaluates hospitals against nationally recognized criteria for maternal care. The health system said the verification gives parents-to-be in northeast Georgia greater confidence in the care available close to home.
