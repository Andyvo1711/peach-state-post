The Port of Savannah handled a record 504,015 twenty-foot equivalent container units (TEUs) in September, the highest volume ever recorded for the month and 3.7 percent more than September 2025, the Georgia Ports Authority reported.

## First-Quarter Results

September brought the authority's fiscal first quarter to more than 1.5 million TEUs, up 2.7 percent and the second-best first quarter in its history. Volumes at the Appalachian Regional Port rose 13 percent over the quarter.

## What Drove It

The authority credited strong consumer spending and an extended peak season. Typhoon-related delays at Asian ports pushed volumes into September, and import loads rose 5.5 percent. The $1.6 billion Ocean Terminal renovation is more than 60 percent complete and will raise the terminal's capacity from 200,000 to 1.75 million TEUs.
