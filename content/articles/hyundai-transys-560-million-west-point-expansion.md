Gov. Brian Kemp announced on October 8 that Hyundai Transys will invest nearly $560 million to expand its manufacturing in West Point, creating 320 jobs.

## About the Company

Hyundai Transys, founded in 1994, makes automotive powertrains and seats. Its West Point facility currently supports about 1,250 jobs. Georgia Power, the City of West Point and Georgia Quick Start were among the project's partners.

## State Reaction

Kemp said the decision reinforces Hyundai's partnership with Georgia and will help Hyundai and Kia expand their capacity here. Jaekyun Choi, president of Hyundai Transys Georgia Powertrain, called it a milestone showing the company's long-term commitment to the state.
