Aviary Beauty + Wellness, an eco-friendly salon and spa with locations in Old Fourth Ward and Summerhill, has added Rescue Spa facials to its menu, FOX 5 Atlanta reported on October 27.

## What's New

Founder Amy Leavell Bransford now offers the Rescue Spa Facial, which carries the New York- and Philadelphia-based brand's Danucera skincare line. Aviary is one of just a handful of places in the U.S. to carry the line.

## The Menu

Aviary's services include facials, waxing, lash and brow tinting, and hair cutting, coloring and styling.
