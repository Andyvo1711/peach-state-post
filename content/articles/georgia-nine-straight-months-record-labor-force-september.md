Georgia's labor force and employment set records for a ninth consecutive month in September, the Georgia Department of Labor reported on October 15.

## The Numbers

The labor force rose by 13,679 to an all-time high of 5,504,015, and employment rose by 10,279 to 5,325,131. The unemployment rate ticked up to 3.3%, 0.1 percentage point lower than a year earlier and 0.9 point below the national rate.

## Lowest Jobless Claims Since 1974

Initial claims fell by 953 to 15,874, the lowest monthly total since April 1974. Over the month, nonfarm jobs fell by 500; over the year they were down 200, with losses concentrated in federal government, professional and technical services, and construction. Labor Commissioner Bárbara Rivera Holmes said the figures show Georgia is the Top State for Talent.
