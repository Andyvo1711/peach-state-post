Georgia State University opened a new research tower in downtown Atlanta on September 22, adding space for the university's growing research enterprise.

## Part of a Long-Range Plan

The tower supports Georgia State's BluePrint to 2033 strategic plan, according to the university's Office of the Provost.

## Research Focus Areas

University leaders said the building will strengthen research across several fields, including biomedical research, health, data science, cybersecurity and sustainability.
