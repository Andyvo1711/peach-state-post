The Council for Quality Growth announced that it will honor Egbert Perry with its 37th Annual Four Pillar Tribute on October 1 in the Georgia Ballroom at the Georgia World Congress Center.

## A Major Annual Gathering

More than 1,400 metro Atlanta and Georgia business and civic leaders were expected to attend and pay tribute to Perry for his contributions to Atlanta's growth and development.

## About the Tribute

The Council for Quality Growth, a business association focused on growth and development issues, gives the Four Pillar Tribute each year to a leader whose work has helped shape the region.
