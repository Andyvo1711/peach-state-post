Construction crews have erected a steel framework for the Dental College of Georgia Savannah just six months after breaking ground, WTOC reported on October 21.

## Progress Check

Augusta University President Russell Keen joined state officials to check on the project, which represents $178 million in state investment and involves multiple universities working together. It is Georgia's second dental school.

## What Comes Next

Once complete, the school will educate 50 dentists and 20 dental hygienists each year. It plans to open in 2028 and bring more than 400 new jobs to Savannah. Keen said the school can draw students from underserved areas and show them a path to health careers in their own backyard, as demand for dental care grows and many dentists near retirement.
