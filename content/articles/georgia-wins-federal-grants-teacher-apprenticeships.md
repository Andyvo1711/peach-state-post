The Georgia Department of Education announced on October 23 that it received two large federal grants to expand teacher apprenticeships across the state.

## Paraprofessional-to-Teacher Pathways

The larger grant, nearly $6 million, aims to expand apprenticeships that move paraprofessionals into teaching roles and to strengthen teacher preparation, mentoring and support for new educators. Together, the two grants total about $10 million, according to the Atlanta Journal-Constitution's reporting.

## Why It Matters

Apprenticeship pathways let school staff already working in classrooms earn certification while staying on the job, a model the department is promoting as one way to ease teacher shortages.
