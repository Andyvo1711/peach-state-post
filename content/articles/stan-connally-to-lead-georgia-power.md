Georgia Power announced on October 30 that Stan Connally, Southern Company's chief operating officer, will become chairman, president and CEO of the utility on January 1.

## Greene's New Role

Kim Greene, who has led Georgia Power through a buildout to support data centers, will become president of Southern Company Operations, overseeing the parent company's grid from Georgia to Mississippi, its power plants and nuclear reactors, and research and commercial operations.

## Familiar Rotation

Southern often rotates senior executives across its businesses. Connally formerly led Gulf Power, now owned by NextEra Energy, and Greene previously headed Southern Company Gas. Southern said its overall operations will become more centralized, while its utilities will keep managing their own relationships with regulators and communities.
