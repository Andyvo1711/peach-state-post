Atlanta Sauna Club, a communal sauna and cold plunge club, plans to open intown in mid-2027, according to its website and Atlanta Magazine's October 30 look at new wellness concepts.

## Where It Is Headed

The operator says it is still scouting locations, focusing on Inman Park, Edgewood, Candler Park and Grant Park. A final site and construction start have not been confirmed.

## Part of a Bigger Wave

Atlanta Magazine counted four new health and wellness concepts, plus one upgraded spa, expected to open around the city by roughly 2027.
