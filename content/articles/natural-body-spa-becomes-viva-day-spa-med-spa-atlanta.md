Natural Body Spa, an Atlanta brand for 37 years, is becoming Viva Day Spa + Med Spa, Viva announced on October 13.

## A Legacy Handoff

Founder Cici Coffee opened the first Natural Body spa in Morningside in 1989. As she considered retirement, she approached Viva's founders, the sisters Maya and Laurie Aroch and Shannon Mouser. Viva, founded in Austin in 2005, has five Texas locations and was voted Best Med Spa in Dallas 2026 by the Dallas Observer.

## First Atlanta Location

The first Viva Atlanta will be at North Highland Avenue NE and University Drive in Morningside/Lenox Park, scheduled to reopen in January 2027. Viva says it will add services including an infrared sauna, couples' rooms and a larger med spa menu. More Natural Body locations are expected to convert over time.
