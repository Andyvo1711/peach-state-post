Downtown Atlanta Inc. has chosen Atlanta Community Food Bank President and CEO Kyle Waide as its next leader, effective in December.

## A Decade at the Food Bank

Waide joined the food bank in 2012 and became its second president and CEO in 2015, succeeding founder Bill Bolling. Under his leadership the organization grew substantially and moved into a 345,000-square-foot facility in East Point.

## Succeeding A.J. Robinson

Downtown Atlanta Inc. is the umbrella organization formed from Central Atlanta Progress and the Atlanta Downtown Improvement District. Waide will succeed A.J. Robinson, who led it for 23 years. "Downtown is central to Atlanta's identity," Waide said in a statement, adding that he hopes to build on Robinson's legacy.
