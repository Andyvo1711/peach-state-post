Northeast Georgia Health System (NGHS) announced on October 22 that it recently received the largest single donation in the organization's history.

## Presentation

A photo released with the announcement shows NGHS President and CEO Matt Hanley, NGHS Foundation President Chris Bray, Foundation employee Maggie James, former NGHS leader LeTrell Simpson and Irene Thomas, the daughter of Anne Thomas.

## Other News From the System

In the same month NGHS announced a new radiology agreement with Union General Health System and added two experienced heart surgeons to its Gainesville practice.
