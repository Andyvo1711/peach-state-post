Georgia Tech and Emory University are investing more than $1 billion in artificial intelligence, the schools said, with plans to expand computing power, train students for the workforce and advance research in Georgia.

## Where the Money Goes

The combined figure includes about $600 million from Emory and more than $575 million from Georgia Tech. Part of Georgia Tech's share supports Nexus, a new AI supercomputer meant to help researchers nationwide tackle projects too large for conventional computers. Its AI Makerspace gives more than 6,000 students each year hands-on experience with the technology, and the university is also building a campuswide computing platform and infrastructure in Tech Square.

Emory's investment supports recruiting and retaining AI experts, health care innovation, and applications in medicine, business, law, the humanities and ethics. Nearly half of its commitment has gone toward talent in Atlanta.

## Workforce Training

Emory's AI + You Georgia program offers free AI education and workforce training, while Georgia Tech is working with schools, technical colleges and employers on new job pathways. Interim President Tim Lieuwen said the goal is to make Georgia a leading technology hub.
