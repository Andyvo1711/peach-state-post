Georgia's unemployment rate fell to 3.2% in August, the lowest since 2024, as the state set new all-time highs for both its labor force and employment, the Georgia Department of Labor reported in mid-September.

## Record Levels

The labor force reached 5,490,319 and employment 5,314,822. The state's rate is 0.9 percentage points below the national rate.

## Sector Movement

Administrative and support services, state government and retail trade recorded the largest job gains in August. Non-durable goods manufacturing and private educational services lost jobs.

*Sources: Georgia Department of Labor; WUGA; Metro Atlanta CEO.*
