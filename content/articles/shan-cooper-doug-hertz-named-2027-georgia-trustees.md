The Georgia Historical Society announced on September 30 that businesswoman and civic leader Shan Cooper and businessman and philanthropist Doug Hertz have been named the 2027 Georgia Trustees, the highest honor the State of Georgia can confer.

## The Honorees

Cooper is a former vice president and general manager at Lockheed Martin and was chief transformation officer at Westrock; she previously chaired the Society's Board of Curators. Hertz is chairman and CEO of United Distributors Inc., was appointed to the Georgia Ports Authority board in 2019 and founded Camp Twin Lakes in 1989.

## Induction

Trustees are appointed annually by the governor, and the title was reestablished in 2008. The 2027 Trustees are scheduled to be inducted at the Trustees Gala in Savannah on April 24, 2027.
