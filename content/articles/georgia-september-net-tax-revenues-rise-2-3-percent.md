Georgia's net tax collections totaled $3.34 billion in September, up 2.3 percent, or $76.7 million, from the same month last year, Gov. Brian Kemp's office announced on October 14.

## Sales and Corporate Taxes Lead

Net sales and use tax collections rose $98.8 million, or 13.3 percent. Gross sales and use tax collections totaled $1.73 billion, up 8.6 percent. Corporate income tax collections rose $37.9 million, or 5.9 percent.

## Individual Income Tax Falls

Individual income tax collections fell $61.3 million, or 4.1 percent, to about $1.42 billion. Year to date, net tax collections stand at $8.41 billion, up $240.2 million, or 2.9 percent, building on a 3.5 percent year-over-year gain in August.

*Sources: Office of Gov. Brian Kemp; Capitol Beat News Service.*
