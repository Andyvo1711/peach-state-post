Bianca Foster opened her 16th bakery franchise location this month, expanding a business that began as a single storefront in downtown Macon into a regional chain spanning Middle Georgia, with plans to reach Metro Atlanta for the first time next year.

Foster started Millbrook Bakehouse South eleven years ago after leaving a corporate marketing career, initially selling bread and pastries out of a small leased space with a handful of employees. Rather than franchising immediately upon early success, Foster spent several years centralizing production at her original Macon facility, a decision she says preserved product consistency as the company scaled.

"I watched other local bakeries franchise too fast and lose the thing that made them special in the first place," Foster said. "We kept baking centralized here in Macon for years longer than most franchise consultants would have recommended, because I wasn't willing to trade quality for speed."

Millbrook Bakehouse South now operates a central production facility that supplies baked goods to all 16 retail locations, allowing individual storefronts to operate with smaller kitchen footprints and lower staffing requirements than a fully independent bakery model. Foster says this structure has kept franchisee costs lower than competing regional bakery franchises.

The company's planned expansion into Metro Atlanta will mark its first location outside Middle Georgia, a milestone Foster says she approached cautiously after watching several regional competitors stumble during rapid expansion.
