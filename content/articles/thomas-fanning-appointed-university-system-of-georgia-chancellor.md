The Board of Regents voted on October 1 to appoint Thomas A. Fanning as the 15th chancellor of the University System of Georgia, effective January 1, 2027.

## A Business Background

Fanning led Southern Company from 2010 to 2023 as president, chief executive and chairman. He succeeds Sonny Perdue, who announced his retirement in April. Regents had named Fanning the sole finalist in mid-September.

The Atlanta Journal-Constitution reported that Fanning has limited higher-education experience but appears to have some faculty support in part because of his business background. The system he will lead includes 25 schools, a multibillion-dollar budget, about 50,000 full-time employees and roughly 382,000 students.

## Early Pushback

On October 6, about 20 University of Georgia students, faculty and staff protested the appointment outside the Georgia Center in Athens. The demonstration was organized by United Campus Workers of Georgia, which raised wage concerns.
