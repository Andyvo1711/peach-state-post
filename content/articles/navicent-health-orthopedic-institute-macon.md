Navicent Health opened a dedicated orthopedic institute in Macon this week, consolidating joint replacement, sports medicine, and physical therapy services into a single outpatient-focused facility designed to reduce wait times for Middle Georgia patients dealing with chronic joint pain.

The institute offers same-day evaluation appointments for new joint pain patients, a departure from the multi-week wait that had previously been standard for an initial orthopedic consultation. Hospital officials say the same-day model was built specifically around outpatient joint replacement surgery, which has grown significantly as surgical techniques and recovery protocols have improved.

"Ten years ago, a hip replacement meant a multi-night hospital stay," said Navicent Health's Chief of Orthopedics. "Now, with the right patient and the right rehab plan, many of our patients go home the same day. This building was designed around that shift, with recovery suites that look more like a hotel room than a hospital ward."

The $55 million facility includes six dedicated orthopedic operating rooms and an on-site physical therapy gym, allowing patients to begin rehabilitation within hours of surgery rather than waiting for a separate outpatient referral. Navicent Health says the institute is expected to handle roughly 1,600 joint replacement procedures annually once fully ramped up.

Hospital officials say Middle Georgia's aging population was a key factor in the decision to invest in expanded orthopedic capacity, with joint replacement demand projected to keep climbing over the next decade.
