Audi plans to build its first U.S. manufacturing plant in Peach County, Georgia, in a project announced October 24 at a rally in Perry. Reports put the investment at about $4 billion, and CBS Atlanta cited $4.2 billion.

## Site and Timeline

The plant would sit on the 1,500-acre Middle Georgia Megasite next to Fort Valley. Grading is expected to begin in 2027 and construction in 2028, with full capacity of 200,000 vehicles a year by 2031, according to the governor's office. Audi's board of directors must still approve the deal.

## Jobs

About 4,500 jobs are expected, with average pay above $63,000 a year, according to Georgia Department of Economic Development Commissioner Pat Wilson. The Technical College System of Georgia will develop curriculum tailored to the plant. Kemp said the project fulfills his pledge to bring jobs to rural Georgia.
