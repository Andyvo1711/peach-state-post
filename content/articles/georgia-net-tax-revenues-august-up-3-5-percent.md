Georgia's net tax collections for August approached $2.50 billion, an increase of $85.4 million, or 3.5%, from August 2025, according to figures released by Gov. Brian Kemp's office.

## What Drove the Gain

- **Individual income tax:** collections approached $1.20 billion, up $40 million (3.5%).
- **Net sales and use tax:** up $44.6 million (5.4%).
- **Local governments:** the adjusted sales tax distribution totaled $911.9 million, up $81.9 million (9.9%).

## A Weak Spot

Corporate income tax collections totaled $18.4 million in August, a decrease of $21.4 million, or 53.8%, from a year earlier.

*Source: Governor's Office monthly net tax revenue release, via Capitol Beat.*
