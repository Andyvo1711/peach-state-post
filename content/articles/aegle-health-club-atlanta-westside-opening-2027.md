Aegle Health Club is scheduled to open in spring 2027 at The Complex, 1737 Ellsworth Industrial Avenue, Suite G, in Atlanta's Westside, and is already accepting membership applications, according to the club's website.

## What It Offers

Atlanta Magazine's October 30 roundup of new wellness concepts describes a 15,500-square-foot facility with a gym and AI-guided training, personal training sessions, and wellness therapies such as red light, compression and sound therapy, along with spa-like treatments. Membership carries a $1,500 initiation and assessment fee.
