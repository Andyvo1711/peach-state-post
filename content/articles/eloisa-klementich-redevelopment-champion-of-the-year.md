Dr. Eloisa Klementich, president and CEO of Invest Atlanta, was named Redevelopment Champion of the Year at the 2026 Metro Atlanta Redevelopment Summit on October 28.

## The Summit

The 16th annual event was held at the John C. Maxwell Leadership Center and presented by Gwinnett's Community Improvement Districts: Evermore, Gateway85, Gwinnett Place, Lilburn and Sugarloaf. Partnership Gwinnett and the Council for Quality Growth co-hosted.

## Recognizing Redevelopment

Awards honored outstanding projects and leaders across metro Atlanta. Kevin Carmichael of Partnership Gwinnett said the recognized work shows what happens when public and private partners align around a shared vision.
