Nutrition Nest, an experiential kitchen and personalized nutrition clinic from longevity brand Preserve Life, opened in Douglasville in early October, Rough Draft Atlanta reported.

## The Facility

It is the first facility to open at Preserve Life's 1,200-acre health and wellness retreat and sits in a 25,000-square-foot mass-timber building. The membership-based day program combines clinical diagnostics, such as body composition and biomarker testing, with culinary medicine and food education led by licensed physicians and nutritionists.

## Recovery and Aesthetics

Atlanta News First's tour highlighted Aesthetics, which tailors skincare recommendations; Biome, an experiential kitchen; a Functional Market; and Circadium, which offers red and infrared light therapy, sauna and hyperbaric oxygen therapy. Co-founder Meena Mulpuri said Douglasville offered land, space and proximity to an international airport.
